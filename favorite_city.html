My Favorite City - Kigali

Kigali, Rwanda

Kigali is the capital city of Rwanda, located in the heart of East Africa. It is known for its beautiful green hills, clean environment, peaceful atmosphere, and modern buildings.

Kigali is also an important center for business, education, technology, and innovation. It continues to grow as a city that attracts visitors, investors, and entrepreneurs from different parts of the world.
